# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Name

Golf Nets Unlimited Website Redesign

---

# Project Vision

Redesign the existing Golf Nets Unlimited website into a premium, modern, elegant, responsive, and highly usable digital experience while preserving all existing business content and information architecture.

This is NOT a rebranding project.

The redesign should elevate the company's perceived value through exceptional UX, premium UI, better storytelling, stronger visual hierarchy, and polished interactions.

The final website should feel like it was designed by a top-tier agency in 2026.

Think Apple-level attention to detail combined with the craftsmanship of luxury architecture and outdoor brands.

---

# Business Overview

Golf Nets Unlimited designs and installs custom golf ball deterrent systems for:

• Residential Homes
• Homeowner Associations (HOA)
• Golf Courses
• Driving Ranges
• Commercial Properties
• Sports Facilities

The company provides engineered netting systems that protect people and property while preserving beautiful landscapes.

The website should communicate:

Engineering Excellence

Premium Craftsmanship

Outdoor Luxury

Trust

Safety

Custom Solutions

Professional Installation

---

# Primary Goal

Create a website that immediately builds trust while making visitors feel they are working with the leading golf netting company in North America.

The redesign should improve:

• First impression

• User engagement

• Readability

• Navigation

without changing the company's messaging.

Understand the relationship between pages.

Preserve all existing business content.
